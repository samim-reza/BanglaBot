BanglaBot voice recording script
================================
How to record
- One speaker, same quiet room, same mic, phone a bit away from the mouth.
- Speak like a polite Dhaka customer-care agent: warm, clear, not news-reader, not rushed.
- One line = one take. Pause 0.5s before and after. If you stumble, redo that line only.
- Questions (?): raise your voice at the end.
- Numbers: say them in Bangla words, as written.
- Target: all of Part A (must), then as much of B–F as you can.
- 250 lines ≈ 40–50 minutes. 350 lines ≈ 1 hour. That is enough for a first fine-tune.

Save files as 001.wav, 002.wav, … matching the numbers below.
22,050 Hz, mono, if you can set that. Otherwise record 48 kHz and we convert.
